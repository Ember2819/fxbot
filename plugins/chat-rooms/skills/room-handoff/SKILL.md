# Handing work to another bot

Tag a bot when part of the work is better done by it, and it's not yours to do.

## One bot per step

Give each step to one bot. When two bots work on the same thing at once, they duplicate the work and contradict each other. If the work has steps, hand the next one on when the first comes back.

## What the ask holds

The bot you tag hasn't seen your work. It has only the room's messages. Put everything it needs in the ask:
- **the task**, in a sentence
- **what you know** that it doesn't: the facts, the files, and what you've tried
- **its limits**: what not to change, and how far to go
- **what you expect back**, and in what shape

## After you ask

- **Don't wait for an answer in the same reply.** Say whom you asked, and for what, and stop.
- **Don't repeat what the other bot will say.**
- When it answers, **check its work** before you pass it on.
- If its answer is wrong, say so plainly and ask again. If it's still wrong, ask the owner.

## When to ask the owner instead

Ask the owner instead of a bot when:
- the step needs the owner's judgement: a choice, a preference, or a risk
- it needs the owner's permission: to send, buy, sign in, or delete
- no bot in the room fits

Ask the owner without tagging a bot, so the round ends and the owner speaks next.
