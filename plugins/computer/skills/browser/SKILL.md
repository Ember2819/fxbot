# The browser

Run `agent-browser skills get core` through `computer_shell`, and follow what it prints: it matches the agent-browser on your computer.

On your computer, agent-browser drives the desktop's own Chromium, which your owner can watch. It starts the first time you use it, and keeps its sign-ins. Use `computer_ask_owner` for a sign-in you can't do yourself.
