fxbot v0.6.0 is out!

https://fxbot.sh/docs/reference/changelog/

Before upgrading: the update adds two settings to your database. Your time zone moves to Settings > General, and the first browser to open fxbot sets it, so check it's right. Then update Dreams and Computer from Plugins.

Changes:

- New Plugin: Messages => Your bots message each other in their own chats, and you watch them work together
- New Plugin: Routines => Saved tasks a bot runs on a schedule, with everything it has, and tells you in its chat
- New Plugin: Permissions => Decide what each bot can do alone: ask you first, a reviewer model, or run everything
- Tag any bot with @ in a chat, as a chip, so the right one is picked even with two of one name (with Messages)
- Messages between bots fold into small pills in the chat, with who and how many received and sent (with Messages)
- A message to a working bot joins what it's doing, instead of waiting for it to finish
- Stop button in a bot's chat
- Up to 32 turns at once, up from 4, with a setting in Settings > General
- One time zone for everything, in Settings > General
- Each plugin has its own tab in Settings
- chat_history now has the whole chat: thinking, tool calls, and their results
- Your messages are readable in every bot's color
- Set up fxbot with your AI agent, from one line on fxbot.sh
- Bug fix: removing a provider stops what's running on it, and its key stops working at once
- Bug fix: a bot stuck on a provider that keeps refusing now stops and says why
- Bug fix: a cancelled turn really stops its computer command
- Bug fix: the computer stays in the bot's panel when fxbot restarts
